# One loop (retired)

The one conversation per machine, decided 2026-11-09 and released as
0.1.56 to 0.1.58, was replaced on 2026-11-10 by the bots, each a
conversation of its own: see [bots.md](bots.md). What it brought
stays there: tasks (§3), the wiki written before a compaction (§4,
§5), the sidebar's marks and the feed's window (§9). Topics, the
views of one conversation by topic, and the `topic` tool are gone. A
machine that ran as one conversation gets its bots back at the first
open of the version after (`rragent.migration`, bots.md §8); the one
conversation stays as a bot, with every topic's turns in its chat.
